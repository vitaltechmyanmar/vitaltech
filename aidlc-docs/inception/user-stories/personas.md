# Vital Tech Myanmar Website - Personas

## Persona 1 - Strategic Technology Leader

### Profile
- **Representative role**: CIO, IT Director, or Head of Technology
- **Context**: Owns technology modernization, service reliability, operational efficiency, and vendor-selection decisions for a Myanmar organization.
- **Digital behavior**: Reviews prospective partners quickly on desktop or mobile, compares capabilities, and shares credible options with executive or procurement stakeholders.

### Goals
- Determine whether Vital Tech Myanmar can solve a defined software, cloud, DevOps, or integration challenge.
- Understand the business value and technical scope of each service without jargon-heavy discovery work.
- Assess fit, delivery confidence, and relevant industry experience before initiating contact.
- Reach a knowledgeable team member by the most convenient direct channel.

### Needs from the Website
- Clear value proposition and immediate service orientation on the Home page.
- Service detail that connects technical capabilities to business outcomes.
- Industry and approved case-study evidence.
- Credible About content, technical perspective through Insights, and easily found phone and email links.

### Success Signal
The leader can articulate Vital Tech Myanmar’s relevant capability and start a direct conversation without having to search for contact details.

## Persona 2 - Technical Solution Evaluator

### Profile
- **Representative role**: Enterprise Architect, IT Manager, Infrastructure Lead, or Engineering Manager
- **Context**: Evaluates the technical feasibility of a prospective partner and informs the technology leader’s decision.
- **Digital behavior**: Looks for service depth, cloud and DevOps competence, integration experience, delivery context, and evidence of a disciplined engineering approach.

### Goals
- Quickly locate the services relevant to the organization’s architecture or delivery problem.
- Understand the relationship between custom software, cloud, DevOps, System Integration, and managed IT support.
- Find approved examples, industry context, and thought leadership that support technical due diligence.

### Needs from the Website
- Scannable service categories with enough detail to understand scope.
- Industry-led capability content and approved case studies when available.
- Insights that demonstrate technical perspective.
- Consistent navigation and readable responsive layouts for research across devices.

### Success Signal
The evaluator can identify a viable service combination and provide an evidence-backed recommendation to the technology leader.
